  • FixedThe Weather app's background is animated again (moving sky, clouds, rain and snow, and the animated figure). Weather rated this phone's processor just under its limit and showed still pictures instead.
  • KnownQuick Settings in the split panel still has a short hitch right at the start of the pull.
  • KnownQuick Share: sending to another device is not confirmed yet. Please report whether nearby devices show up and a transfer goes through.
  • FixedOpening Quick Settings stutters much less, in both the split and the combined panel. The tiles were being redrawn three times on every open.
  • FixedIn the split panel, scrolled Quick Settings tiles no longer slide over the clock and the buttons at the top.
  • KnownQuick Settings in the split panel still has a short hitch right at the start of the pull.
  • FixedWi-Fi no longer disconnects every time Quick Share looks for nearby devices. A Galaxy S26 Ultra component for sharing with Apple devices needed a second Wi-Fi radio this phone doesn't have, and cut the connection on each search.
  • FixedGallery's Private album works. Moving photos and videos into it failed with "Unable to move" because the encrypted storage behind it was switched off. It needs a screen lock (PIN, pattern or password).
  • RemovedQuick Share to Apple devices (AirDrop), which can't work on this hardware.
  • FixedSmall layout fix in Software update.
  • NewSettings > K4_UN1CA Settings > Software update. Checks for new builds, downloads them in the background (resumes after a lost connection, checks the file before installing) and installs them through TWRP, then restarts into the system on its own. You get a notification when a new build is out. Needs about 8 GB of free storage.
  • FixedWhen an install fails in TWRP, the screen now explains what happened, whether the phone was changed and what to do next.
  • FixedMoving photos and videos from Gallery to Secure Folder no longer fails with "Unable to move".
  • FixedPhotos taken with the camera shortcut on the lock screen open when you tap the thumbnail, without unlocking first. Only photos from that session are shown.
  • UpdatedSamsung Internet 31.0.0.81.
  • Fixed(9.3.133) Pulling down Quick Settings in the split layout is smoother. The next row of tiles now ends in a hard edge at the bottom of the panel instead of fading out.
  • Fixed(9.3.132) Settings no longer crashes when you turn off "AI privacy alerts" in Security and privacy > Alert center. The switch is hidden because this ROM doesn't include the service behind it.
  • Fixed(9.3.132) Opening the notification panel and Quick Settings is smoother. The CPU now gets a short speed boost when you touch the screen.
  • New(9.3.132) PUBG Mobile, BGMI, Call of Duty Mobile and Mobile Legends now see the phone as an Infinix GT 30 Pro, for higher graphics and frame rate options. Other games keep their existing profiles.
⚡ How to Flash (TWRP)
  1. Download the ROM zip & verify the SHA-256 checksum.
  2. Boot into TWRP custom recovery on your Galaxy A52s 5G.
  3. Wipe Data, Cache, and Dalvik (or Format Data if switching from another ROM).
  4. Flash the downloaded ROM zip file.
  5. Reboot to System & enjoy One UI 9!
